About Me

I am a visual artist and illustrator focused on atmospheric storytelling, editorial aesthetics and emotionally driven imagery.

My work explores themes of femininity, memory, intimacy and quiet symbolism, often inspired by fashion photography, cinema and contemporary illustration.

Currently studying illustration at the University of Hertfordshire, I aim to create elegant visual narratives that combine fine art sensibility with modern digital aesthetics.
